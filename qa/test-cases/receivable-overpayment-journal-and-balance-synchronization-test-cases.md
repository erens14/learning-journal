# Test Cases: Receivable Overpayment Journal and Balance Synchronization

**Category:** Quality Assurance, Financial Accounting, Integration, and Regression
**Target Scope:** Receivable Payment — Bank Posting, Overpaid Allocation, Remaining Balance, and PPh Synchronization
**Environment:** Target regression environment

> Documented scenarios, references, and data in this repository are sanitized for portfolio use. Customer identities, bank-receipt numbers, transaction dates, amounts, and internal ticket references are generalized or omitted.

## Preconditions

- Use an active receivable with a known outstanding amount and a payment scenario where PPh has been deducted.
- Prepare a bank mutation whose received amount exceeds the receivable balance, producing a known overpaid amount.
- Record the expected bank COA, `Titipan Uang Angkutan` COA, corresponding `Piutang Usaha Angkutan` COA, PPh value, receivable balance, and overpaid difference before execution.
- Use an authorized finance user and a clean transaction that has not already been posted.

## Requirements

**Feature goal:** Post an overpaid receivable payment once, settle the receivable, classify the excess amount correctly, and synchronize the payment and tax values across all related views.

**Key rules / constraints:** The bank COA must appear once and match the bank mutation. The overpaid reclassification must debit `Titipan Uang Angkutan` and credit the corresponding `Piutang Usaha Angkutan` account. The posted journal must remain balanced, the settled receivable must show `Remain = 0`, and the deducted PPh must be reflected consistently after payment.

## Execution Notes

- The tester confirmed that all Jira-reported overpayment problems covered by `TC-ROP-001` through `TC-ROP-006` passed retesting.
- `TC-ROP-007` and `TC-ROP-008` remain `NOT RUN` because persistence-after-reload and standard-payment regression results were not included in the supplied confirmation.
- Use sanitized evidence for the bank mutation, payment, journal, receivable list/detail, overpaid record, and PPh comparison.
- If a case fails, record the exact observed account, debit/credit direction, value difference, and screen where the inconsistency appears.
- Confirm the expected COA mapping with the approved finance requirement before marking the accounting scenarios as passed.
