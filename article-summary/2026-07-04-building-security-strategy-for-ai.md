# Building a Security Strategy for AI

**Source:** The Hacker News  
**Author:** Austin O'Saben - Kaseya  
**Link:** [Building a Security Strategy for AI-Powered Ransomware Attacks](https://thehackernews.com/expert-insights/2026/06/building-security-strategy-for-ai.html)  

---

## Summary

The article argues that AI assets belong in the organization's existing security program. Its scope includes models, datasets, prompts, APIs, and inference pipelines, with security responsibilities shared by developers, security teams, and business stakeholders.

The risks described include prompt injection, model manipulation, data leakage, insecure APIs, supply-chain exposure, and internal or external misuse. The proposed response combines asset visibility, identity and access management, governance, and monitoring throughout development and operation.

## Key Takeaways

An AI inventory needs to identify the data and permissions available to each workload, along with the people responsible for its use. Monitoring and access controls need to cover the model's interactions with other systems.

## Lesson Learned

Include AI-specific risks in design reviews and ongoing risk assessment. Infrastructure protection alone does not establish that prompts, training data, or inference outputs are handled safely.
